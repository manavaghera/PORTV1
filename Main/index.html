  1. 01

    Chronus Research

    My research bet: AI that remembers what it learned and can show where it learned it. Runs entirely on your machine — encrypted search is next.

    RAG · Sentence-BERT · ChromaDB↗
  2. 02

    Health Risk Engine Kaggle top 1%

    Three gradient-boosting models stacked into one, on 690K rows where 86% sit in a single class. Validated step by step, it went from 0.876 to 0.952 balanced accuracy and finished top 1% of 3,355 teams.

    LightGBM · XGBoost · CatBoost · Optuna↗
  3. 03

    AutonomousContent

    A newsroom of AI agents: a researcher, a writer and a validator that runs a four-stage quality check and sends weak drafts back. Published posts score 82–89/100 on automated checks, on free-tier models at zero cost.

    LangGraph · Python · OpenRouter↗
  4. 04

    NewsSense

    An AI news platform: every hour it pulls RSS and official sources, merges duplicates, checks AI-written claims against the evidence and publishes only what holds up. 211 backend tests.

    FastAPI · React · PostgreSQL · Docker↗
  5. 05

    Clip Studio

    Finds the Twitch clips that are taking off, ranked by views per hour, and cuts the best into vertical Shorts with burned-in captions. Whisper transcribes; an optional local LLM rates the moments. 127 tests.

    Python · Streamlit · Whisper · ffmpeg↗
  6. 06

    Fasal AI

    A farmer points a phone at a leaf and gets the diagnosis in real time. Ask out loud in Hindi or Gujarati — it works offline on budget Android phones.

    YOLOv8 · TensorFlow · Whisper · ONNX
  7. 07

    DermaLense.AI

    Skin-spot checks right in your browser. The photo never leaves your device — privacy by design.

    JavaScript · Computer vision↗
